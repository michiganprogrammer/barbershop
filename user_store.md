# Barber App

## Overview
A web app that allows customers to book 
appointments with barbers

## User Stories

### Customer
- As a customer I want to see nearest store
- As a customer I want to book an appointment
- As a customer I want to cancel a booking
- As a customer I want to wait in line
- As a customer I want to see wait time
- As a customer I need to pay
- As a customer I want to see barber rating
- As a customer I want to see price of service
- As a customer I want to get email receipt
- As a customer I need to give a rating after service
- As a customer I need to make a profile
- As a customer I want to book as a guest
- 

### Barber
- As a barber I want to see my schedule...
- As a barber I want to block off time...
